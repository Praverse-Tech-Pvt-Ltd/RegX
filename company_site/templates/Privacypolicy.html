1. Introduction

iPharmaAI respects the privacy and confidentiality of our clients, users, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, protect, and manage information when users access our website, SaaS-based application platform, and related services.

iPharmaAI is designed to help pharmaceutical and regulatory teams simplify regulatory operations, strengthen FDA inspection readiness, accelerate ANDA workflows, and proactively manage compliance risk using AI-driven inspection intelligence, audit analytics, regulatory checklists, and CTD documentation support.

By using our website or SaaS platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

A. Account and User Information

When a client or authorized user accesses the iPharmaAI platform, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Company name
  • Business email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Job title or department, if provided
  • Login credentials or system access details
  • User role and access permission information

iPharmaAI uses a controlled single sign-in access model. User access may be browser-specific and/or system-specific for security purposes. Users cannot reset passwords directly through the platform. Password reset or access-related requests must be submitted to iPharmaAI by email.

B. Platform Usage Information

We may collect information about how users access and use the platform, including:

  • Login activity
  • Login token
  • IP address

This information helps us maintain platform security, improve performance, and support authorized client usage.

C. Client-Provided Information

Clients may provide business, regulatory, quality, technical, or operational information when using the platform or requesting support.

  • Regulatory questions
  • Audit or inspection readiness needs
  • ANDA or CTD documentation-related inputs
  • Checklist usage preferences
  • Support requests
  • Feedback or product improvement suggestions

Clients are responsible for ensuring that any information they provide to iPharmaAI is accurate and appropriate for use within the platform.

D. Website and Communication Information

When visitors contact us through the website, email, demo request, or inquiry form, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Business email address
  • Company name
  • Phone number
  • Message content
  • Service interest or inquiry details

We may also collect limited technical information through cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and user experience.

3. Information We Do Not Intend to Collect

iPharmaAI is not designed to collect patient-level health information, protected health information, medical records, personal clinical data, or sensitive personal information unless specifically agreed in writing under a separate contract.

Users should not upload or submit confidential patient data, personal health information, or highly sensitive information unless iPharmaAI has expressly agreed to receive and process such information under appropriate legal and contractual safeguards.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide authorized access to the iPharmaAI SaaS platform
  • Manage user accounts and login permissions
  • Support single sign-in and browser/system-specific access controls
  • Provide AuditIQ, RegX, ANDA CTD SmartTemplate and related services
  • Allow users to access, search, review, and download permitted content
  • Respond to inquiries, support requests, and password/access reset requests
  • Improve platform functionality, security, and user experience
  • Monitor authorized use and prevent unauthorized access
  • Provide product updates, service communications, and administrative notices
  • Maintain internal records, audit trails, and compliance documentation
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, contractual, or security obligations

We do not sell client data or personal information.

5. Use of Client Data

Client data is treated as confidential business information. iPharmaAI uses client data only for legitimate business, service, support, security, and contractual purposes.

We do not use client confidential information for unrelated marketing, resale, or disclosure to third parties except as described in this Privacy Policy or as agreed in writing with the client.

Where AI-enabled tools or analytics are used within the platform, client-provided data is handled in accordance with applicable access controls, security safeguards, and contractual obligations.

iPharmaAI does not intentionally use client confidential information to train public AI models unless expressly agreed in writing.

6. Disclosure of Information

A. Authorized Service Providers

We may share limited information with trusted service providers who support our website, hosting, software operations, security, analytics, email, or technical support.

B. Legal or Regulatory Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, regulation, court order, subpoena, government request, or other legal process.

C. Security and Protection

We may disclose information if necessary to protect the security, rights, property, or safety of iPharmaAI, iCretegy, our clients, users, or others.

D. With Client Consent

We may share information when the client or user provides consent or instructs us to do so.

7. Data Security

iPharmaAI takes data security seriously and uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Our security practices may include:

  • Controlled user access
  • Single sign-in access model
  • Browser and/or system-specific access restrictions
  • Password reset handled through direct email request
  • Role-based access control, where applicable
  • Secure hosting and access monitoring
  • Technical logs for security and troubleshooting
  • Data backup and recovery practices
  • Confidentiality controls for internal access
  • Use of secure communication methods where appropriate

Although we take reasonable steps to protect information, no website, SaaS platform, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting their login credentials and immediately notifying iPharmaAI of any suspected unauthorized access.

8. Password Reset and Access Control

For security purposes, users cannot reset their passwords directly through the platform. If a user forgets a password, changes a device, changes a browser, or experiences access issues, the user must contact iPharmaAI by email at: icretools@icretegy.com

iPharmaAI may verify the user’s identity, company authorization, browser, device, or account status before restoring access.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website performance, remember user preferences, analyse traffic, and support secure access. Users may adjust browser settings to disable cookies; however, some website or platform features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain user accounts, support contractual obligations, comply with legal or regulatory requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When information is no longer required, we may delete, archive, anonymize, or securely retain it in accordance with our internal policies and applicable requirements.

Depending on applicable law and location, users or clients may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Request information about how data is used or disclosed
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To submit a privacy-related request, please contact: icretools@icretegy.com. We may need to verify the identity and authority of the requester before processing any request.

12. Regional Privacy Notice

iPharmaAI serves business users and clients in multiple regions. Privacy rights and legal requirements may vary depending on the user’s location and applicable law. Where required by applicable privacy laws, users may have certain rights regarding their personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how their data is collected, used, stored, or disclosed.

iPharmaAI handles personal information and client data for lawful business purposes, including platform access, account management, client support, service delivery, security monitoring, product improvement, and contractual administration. iPharmaAI does not sell personal information. We also do not intentionally disclose client confidential information to third parties except as necessary to provide services, support platform operations, comply with legal obligations, protect security, or as otherwise agreed with the client.

Because iPharmaAI may serve users in different locations, information may be accessed, stored, or processed in regions other than the user’s location. Where required, iPharmaAI uses reasonable safeguards designed to protect information during such processing. Users may submit privacy-related questions, access requests, correction requests, deletion requests, grievance requests, or other data-related concerns by contacting: icretools@icretegy.com. iPharmaAI may verify the identity, authority, and company affiliation of the requester before processing any privacy or data-related request.
