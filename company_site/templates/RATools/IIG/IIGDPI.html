IMPORTANCE OF IIG PROPORTIONALITY CLEARANCE - DPIs TEMPLATE

         In the development and approval process for Dry Powder Inhalation (DPI) products, IIG Clearance plays a crucial role in ensuring that the inactive ingredients used are within the acceptable limits established by the FDA, which is essential for maintaining product safety, efficacy, and patient tolerance.


        The IIG Clearance template helps ensure that all inactive ingredients in the formulation are accurately assessed against FDA standards. This ensures that the formulation does not exceed established safety thresholds, reducing the risk of regulatory setbacks and potential adverse effects.


       This template ensures that all inactive ingredients’ IIG evaluations are conducted and meet IIG criteria which support a smoother regulatory review, reducing the likelihood of deficiencies and facilitating a more efficient approval process.


       The Maximum Daily Dose (MDD) is a key parameter in pharmaceutical development, ensuring patient safety and regulatory compliance. It is determined using information from the Reference Listed Drug (RLD) package insert available on Drugs@FDA , the Orange Book, or DailyMed

Inactive Ingredient Approval – Three Tier Approach (Inhalation)

General Points to consider when sending the composition for RA evaluation:
  1. Please do not mention only the trade name of the excipient. Please mention Pharmacopoeial name followed by trade name of any excipient (ex: Lactose Monohydrate, NF (Trade Name if any)).
  2. Please include the actual function of each excipient.
  3. Specifying RM code will help RA to find vendor name to check the availability of tox report/DMF LoAs at our end when there is any IIG issue.
  4. The Formulation team needs to mark the “project phase” for our understanding when sending the composition for evaluation.
  5. Provide the Exact Grade / Form of the particular Excipient [E.g.: Flavoring Agents will be available in different grades; & Sodium Citrate USP has different forms like Anhydrous, Dihydrate with different CAS numbers.]
  6. Update and send Revised IIG clearance form to Regulatory, if there would be any changes to the formulation composition once after the IIG is Approved.
  7. Please make sure the name of excipient is harmonized in IIG form, specifications and batch records.
  8. If there is Controlled Correspondence approved for Q1/Q2, then please include exactly the same formulation. Do not change anything, even hydrate form of excipient.
  9. MDE is a better reference, MDE should considered rather than maximum potency for those excipients for which MDE is available.

Note to RA: Whenever any excipient’s name is not available in IIG database or IIG limit is lower than the proposed formulation quantity, please check FDA’s Substance Registration System (SRS) [http://fdasis.nlm.nih.gov/srs/srs.jsp] for that particular excipient’s synonyms, Unique Ingredient Identifier # (UNII) and also Chemical Abstractive number (CAS number). If this excipient’s synonym is available with the higher IIG limit for the specific route of administration, then IIG limit for that excipient (Synonym) can be also considered to justify the proposed quantity in our formulation.
