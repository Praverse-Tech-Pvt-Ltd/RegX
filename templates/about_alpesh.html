About Alpesh Patel

Regulatory leadership built for modern pharma.

Alpesh Patel is a pharmaceutical industry leader with over 30+ years of experience, driving regulatory excellence, developing AI-enabled tools, supporting international expansion, advancing leadership development, and delivering cost-synergy initiatives that enhance speed to market and enable sustainable growth across pharmaceutical organizations.

30+ Years of pharmaceutical leadership experience
500+ ANDA/505(b)(2) submissions supported
13+ Dosage forms across complex portfolios
18+ Years serving as a primary FDA liaison

During his 23+ year tenure at Amneal Pharmaceuticals (S&P: AMRX), he led and supported 500+ ANDA/505(b)(2) submissions across 13+ dosage forms and 17+ therapeutic areas, significantly contributing to the company's growth in the global generics and branded pharmaceutical markets.

Alpesh Patel has held several senior leadership roles at Amneal Pharmaceuticals for over 23 years, including Senior Vice President, Global Regulatory Affairs, along with key leadership positions in EVP, Strategic Excellence & Technical Enhancement (SETE), Business Transformation & Innovation (BTI), Leadership & Enhancement (L&E), and President of SIE (Strategic International Expansion). These initiatives operated as a Knowledge Process Organization (KPO) focused on enhancing operational efficiency, strengthening technical capabilities, and driving organizational performance.

He has successfully introduced gamification-driven programs to elevate excellence in Quality and Operational Performance across the organization. Additionally, he is the founder of iPharmaAI, a SaaS and AI-powered platform for US FDA 483 observation analytics, enabling companies to proactively identify compliance risks and significantly reduce the likelihood of 483 observations, OAI classifications, and warning letters.

Alpesh has also served as a primary FDA liaison for 18+ years working closely with multiple technical committees, including SaRB, QbD, TEC, DMF, GDUFA, OPQ, Quality Matrix, Compliance, and the Office of Pharmaceutical Quality (OPF) from 2005 to 2022, providing comprehensive end-to-end regulatory, compliance strategy and guidance.

He has conducted numerous MOSAIC leadership and technical workshops ("Excellence in Execution") for organizations such as Amneal, Cipla, Harman, Cosette, DifGen, BDR, and Aveva - empowering professionals across all levels to strengthen collaboration, build leadership capabilities, and drive high-performance cultures.
